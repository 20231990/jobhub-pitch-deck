# JobHub Platform Pitch Deck

## Overview
This repository contains the **pitch deck presentation** for the JobHub Platform. The pitch deck outlines the platform's features, target audience, market opportunity, and development roadmap. It is intended for investors, stakeholders, and potential partners.

---


---

## Platform Summary

**JobHub** is a digital job platform designed to connect job seekers with potential employers efficiently. The platform includes:

- User registration and login
- Job listings, creation, and management
- CV uploads and notifications
- Admin and user dashboards
- Real-time job application tracking

---

## Pitch Deck Content

The pitch deck includes:

1. **Introduction & Problem Statement** – Overview of the job market challenges.
2. **Solution** – How JobHub solves these problems.
3. **Market Opportunity** – Target audience and market potential.
4. **Product Features** – Key functionalities of the platform.
5. **Technology Stack** – MERN stack, database, and deployment details.
6. **Roadmap** – Planned future updates and improvements.
7. **Team** – Roles and responsibilities of the development team.
8. **Financials / Business Model** – Revenue streams and sustainability.
9. **Contact Information** – For investors and collaborators.
